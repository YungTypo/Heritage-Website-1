Insurance Claim Assistance

We help you navigate the claims process from inspection to payout.

Filing an insurance claim after storm or disaster damage can be confusing and overwhelming. You shouldn't have to figure it out alone. Heritage Roofing & Restoration has walked homeowners through the process many times, and we make it as straightforward as possible — with honest documentation and clear guidance at every step.

We document the damage your adjuster needs.

Claims live and die on documentation. We provide the photos, measurements, and damage assessments your adjuster expects, organized so nothing legitimate gets missed or overlooked.

We'll meet your adjuster on-site.

When it helps, we walk the property with your adjuster so everyone is looking at the same damage and working from the same scope. It keeps the process moving and reduces back-and-forth.

From first inspection to final payout.

We stay with you from the initial free inspection through the repairs themselves, and we can coordinate the roof repair or replacement once your claim is approved.

Honest, homeowner-first guidance.

We're a roofing contractor, not a public adjuster or law firm — so our role is to document the damage truthfully and do excellent repair work, not to make promises about your policy. We'll always give you a straight answer about what we see.

Frequently Asked

Do you handle the insurance paperwork for me?

We provide the documentation, photos, and damage assessments and support you through the process. You file with your insurer; we make sure the damage is captured accurately.

What if my claim is denied?

We can re-document the damage and help you understand your options. We can't guarantee an outcome, but thorough documentation gives a legitimate claim its best footing.

Will filing a claim raise my rates?

That depends entirely on your insurer and policy, and we can't speak for your carrier — it's a good question to ask your agent directly.

Is the damage assessment free?

Yes. The inspection and damage assessment are free and no-pressure.
