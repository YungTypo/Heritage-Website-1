More Services

Patios, crawlspaces, tree removal — and more on the way.

Beyond roofing and restoration, Heritage handles a growing list of home projects. We're a local company building a full-service reputation one honest job at a time — so if you're not sure whether we can help, just ask.

Patios & outdoor living.

We build and repair patios that hold up to the weather and add usable space to your home. Whether you're starting fresh or fixing settling and cracking, we'll give you a straight assessment.

Crawlspace repair & encapsulation.

A damp crawlspace affects everything above it — air quality, energy bills, and the structure itself. We repair and encapsulate crawlspaces to keep moisture out and your home healthier.

Storm-damaged tree removal.

When a storm leaves a tree compromised or down on your home, we can remove it and handle the resulting roof repair — one call instead of three.

More on the way.

We're expanding into windows, doors, and larger renovation projects soon. Tell us what you need and we'll let you know what we can help with today and what's coming.
