Storm & Disaster Restoration

Wind, hail, fire, and water damage — handled start to finish.

Storms don't wait for a convenient time, and neither do we. When wind, hail, fire, or water damages your home, the speed of your response decides how much worse it gets. Heritage Roofing & Restoration is available 24/7 to secure your home, assess the damage, and start the path back to normal.

Fast response limits the damage.

A lifted shingle or a small breach lets water travel — into decking, insulation, drywall, and eventually the rooms you live in. Acting quickly with a tarp or temporary repair often saves thousands in secondary damage. That's why we answer the phone day or night and move fast when a storm rolls through Middle Tennessee or Southern Kentucky.

We assess the full scope — not just what's visible.

Storm damage hides. Hail bruises that aren't obvious from the ground, wind creases that break the seal on a shingle, granule loss that shortens a roof's life — we look for all of it, on the roof and around the property, and document it thoroughly.

Documentation your insurance adjuster needs.

We photograph and itemize the damage and put together the documentation your insurer expects, then walk the property with your adjuster when it helps. Learn more about our insurance claim assistance.

24/7 storm response across the region.

From the Greater Nashville area to Bowling Green, KY, we cover 49+ communities across Middle Tennessee and Southern Kentucky. Wind, hail, fire, or water — call us any time.

Frequently Asked

How fast can you respond after a storm?

We run 24/7 storm-damage response. Call (615) 670-3132 any time and we'll move to secure your home and assess the damage.

What kinds of storm damage do you handle?

Wind, hail, fire, and water damage to roofs and exteriors — from emergency tarping through full restoration.

Will you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We document the damage and support your claim from the first inspection through to payout — see Insurance Claim Assistance.

What should I do right after storm damage?

Stay safe and avoid the damaged area, photograph what you can from the ground, and call us — we'll handle the inspection and temporary protection.
