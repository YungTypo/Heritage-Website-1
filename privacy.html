Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Heritage Roofing & Restoration ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, contact us, or call, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, property location, and any details you share about your project. We may also collect basic, non-identifying analytics about how visitors use our site.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to your requests, schedule inspections and work, assist with insurance claims, and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We may share information with trusted partners who help us operate our business — such as crews, suppliers, financing providers, or your insurance company at your direction — only as needed to deliver the services you request.

Cookies & analytics

Our site may use cookies and similar technologies to understand site usage and improve your experience. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices

You may opt out of marketing messages at any time, and you can request that we update or delete your information by contacting us.
