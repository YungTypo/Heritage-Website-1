About Heritage Roofing & Restoration

Locally owned, licensed and insured, and built on telling homeowners the truth.

Local, responsive, and built on trust

Heritage Roofing & Restoration was founded in 2026 on a simple idea: homeowners deserve a roofing company that shows up, tells them the truth, and does the work right the first time.

We're a locally owned company based in Gallatin, Tennessee, serving homeowners across Middle Tennessee and Southern Kentucky — from roof repairs and full replacements to storm-damage restoration and insurance-claim support. We're available 24/7 for storm response, because storm damage doesn't wait for business hours.

We're licensed in Tennessee and fully insured, and we're building our reputation the only way that lasts: one honest job at a time.

Meet the owners

Heritage is owned and operated by Nathan Hillis and Jacob Castillo — two co-owners who are personally involved in the work and the people behind it. When you call Heritage, you're dealing with the owners, not a call center.
